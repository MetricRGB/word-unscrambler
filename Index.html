How to Maximize Your Scores in Scrabble

  • High-Value Short Words: Memorize obscure two-letter and three-letter words containing high-point letters like X, Z, Q, and J (e.g., ZA, QI, XI).
  • Leverage Blank Tiles Wisely: Use the wildcard search feature on this utility to determine all possible hook words you can build with blank tiles.
  • Prefix and Suffix Deconstruction: Isolate common roots like UN-, RE-, -ING, or -ED to dramatically reduce the permutations of your rack letters.
